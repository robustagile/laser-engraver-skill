## Acceptance criteria

- The cut goes through the anodic layer into the metal, giving a bright mark against the coating,
  and does so consistently — no patches where the coating survives and reads as a different
  colour.
- Depth into the aluminium below the coating stays under 0.05 mm unless the job asks for more.
- No lifted, crazed or discoloured coating around the stroke edges.
- Legible down to 1 mm character height.

## How to verify

Depth gauge from the anodised surface on a coupon from the same anodising batch, and a look under
diffuse light for patchiness — an under-powered pass leaves a mark that looks even indoors and
mottled in daylight.
