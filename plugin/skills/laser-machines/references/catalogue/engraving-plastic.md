## Acceptance criteria

- Depth is deliberate and repeatable, and the material is removed rather than melted aside: no
  swollen lip standing proud of the surface.
- No stringing, webbing or re-solidified spatter inside or around the cut.
- The colour of the cut is consistent — a plastic that carbonises unevenly reads as a dirty mark
  rather than an engraved one.
- No deformation of thin sections from accumulated heat.

## How to verify

Depth gauge and a fingernail across the edge for the lip. Burn the cell twice with a pause
between and compare: plastics show heat accumulation as a difference between the first and second
run that metals do not.
