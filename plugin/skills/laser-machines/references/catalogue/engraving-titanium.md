## Acceptance criteria

- Depth between 0.02 and 0.05 mm — enough to feel with a fingernail, not enough to weaken a thin
  part or to show through the back of sheet stock.
- Uniform fill: at arm's length no stripe pattern from the line interval is visible in a solid
  area.
- No burr at the stroke edges that a fingernail catches on.
- Legible at the size the job actually uses, down to 1 mm character height.
- No brown or straw discolouration halo around the marked area.

**Not on hand.** Where the owner has none of this material, the entry stays a `candidate` on
researched numbers and never reaches `verified` — the coverage map shows it as a gap, and the
agent must say plainly that nothing here was ever burned before anyone runs it on a part
(R-R18).

## How to verify

Burn the standard cell on a coupon of the same titanium and finish as the job. Measure depth with a
depth gauge at three points in a solid area, and take the shallowest as the result. Judged by eye in diffuse daylight, straight on and at 45 degrees. A photograph is triage only, never the basis for `verified` (R-R11).
