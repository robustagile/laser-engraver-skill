## Acceptance criteria

- Depth reaches the stated target at the shallowest point, within -0 / +30 %.
- No heat colour left in the surrounding metal beyond 0.5 mm from the cut, or none at all where
  the job says so — titanium takes oxide colour readily and a deep cut heats the area badly.
- Even floor; no dished centre.

**Not on hand.** Where the owner has none of this material, the entry stays a `candidate` on
researched numbers and never reaches `verified` — the coverage map shows it as a gap, and the
agent must say plainly that nothing here was ever burned before anyone runs it on a part
(R-R18).

## How to verify

Depth gauge on a coupon burned in the same session. Inspect the surround under diffuse light for
oxide colour — a straw or blue ring is the signal that the pass structure is dumping too much
heat, whatever the depth reads.
