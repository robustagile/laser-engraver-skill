## Acceptance criteria

- The mark reads white to light grey at a glance in normal room light, and is even across the
  whole marked area.
- No measurable depth: a depth gauge reads nothing, and a fingernail drawn across the edge
  catches on nothing. Etching textures the surface; it does not remove it.
- No brown, straw or bronze discolouration halo — heat colour means the settings are into
  annealing territory, which is a different work with a different goal.
- Edges stay sharp at 1 mm character height.

## How to verify

Depth gauge over the mark to confirm nothing was removed, then judge by eye in diffuse daylight,
straight on and at 45 degrees. A photograph is triage only, never the basis for `verified`
(R-R11). Use a coupon of the same stainless-steel and the same finish as the job: surface finish changes
the result more than the alloy does.
