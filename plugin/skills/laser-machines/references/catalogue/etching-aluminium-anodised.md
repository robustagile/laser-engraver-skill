## Acceptance criteria

- The mark is white to light grey against the coating, even across the whole area, with the
  anodic layer bleached or removed and the metal below **not** cut into.
- No measurable depth: a depth gauge reads nothing and a fingernail catches on nothing.
- No brown or bronze cast — that is the coating being burnt rather than bleached.
- Edges stay sharp at 1 mm character height; the coating does not craze around them.

## How to verify

Depth gauge to confirm nothing was cut, then judge colour by eye in daylight, straight on and at
45 degrees. Coating thickness varies between anodising batches, so the coupon must come from the
same batch as the job.
