## Acceptance criteria

- Legible light mark with even coverage over the whole marked area.
- No spalling or flaking at the stroke edges, and no cracks radiating from corners.
- Consistent across the piece — natural stone varies in hardness within one tile.
- Dust brushes off; nothing needs scraping.

## How to verify

Brush clean, then judge coverage by eye in daylight and check the corners with a loupe. Burn the
same cell at two separated places on the same piece: the variation within the material is the
thing being tested, not a single good cell.
