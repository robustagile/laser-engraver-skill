## Acceptance criteria

- Even light mark over the whole area, no flaking at the edges, no cracks from corners.
- Consistent between two separated places on the same piece.
- Dust brushes off without scraping.

## How to verify

Brush clean and judge in daylight; loupe at the corners. Burn at two separated places on the same
piece and compare.
