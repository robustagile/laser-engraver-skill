## Acceptance criteria

- Contrast without deformation: the marked area reads clearly and the surface stays flat.
- No swollen lip, no soot that wipes away with a cloth, no stringing.
- The effect is repeatable on the second run — plastics that foam are sensitive to heat left
  from the first.

## How to verify

Wipe with isopropanol and look again: a mark that fades was soot, not a mark. Run the same cell
twice with a pause and compare.
