## Acceptance criteria

- Depth **at least 0.0762 mm (0.003 in)** measured **from the anodised surface**, at the
  shallowest point of the shallowest character.
- Character height **at least 1.5875 mm (1/16 in)**, measured on the part.
- The cut goes **through the anodic layer into the aluminium** and is unambiguously a cut, not a
  bleached mark in the coating that happens to read dark.
- Edges of the anodic layer are not lifted or crazed around the cut.
- **No bare bright metal left exposed.** Aluminium does not anneal to black, so the stainless
  route is unavailable and there are two others, both legitimate and each with its own entry:
  `whitening-engraved-floor-aluminium`, a light etching pass over the same geometry that turns
  the floor matte white, and `ink-bonding-aluminium`, a laser-bonding ink filled into the cut.
  The first is laser-only and adds nothing to the part; the second gives a genuinely black mark
  and brings a consumable and a wash step with it.
- Whichever is used, the **depth criterion above is measured before the contrast step and holds
  after it** — and on a regulatory mark the cut alone must remain compliant and legible without
  the ink, which can wear off.

The anodic layer is typically 0.01 to 0.05 mm thick, so a mark that only removes the coating can
look convincing and still be an order of magnitude short of the depth required. The two figures
above must be confirmed against the regulatory cache before a compliance job (R-C2).

## How to verify

Depth gauge, from the anodised surface, on the part or on a coupon of the same anodising batch —
coating thickness varies between batches and it is part of the measurement. Character height with
calipers. Both measurements mandatory.
