## Acceptance criteria

- The named colour, uniform across the whole marked area and across the field.
- Zero material removal.
- Repeatable run to run.
- Survives an isopropanol wipe and light rubbing.

Titanium reaches colour over a narrower window than stainless, and the window differs between
grade 2 and grade 5 enough that a recipe verified on one is a candidate, not a result, on the
other.

**Not on hand.** Where the owner has none of this material, the entry stays a `candidate` on
researched numbers and never reaches `verified` — the coverage map shows it as a gap, and the
agent must say plainly that nothing here was ever burned before anyone runs it on a part
(R-R18).

## How to verify

Swatch at field centre and two opposite corners, compared in daylight; a second run to confirm
repeatability; isopropanol wipe. Record the grade — a recipe carried across grades must go back
to `candidate` (R-R4, R-R7).
