## Acceptance criteria

- Depth follows the design: the deepest area reaches its target within ±20 %, and areas designed
  shallower are proportionally shallower rather than all bottoming out together.
- Floors at each level are even, and the steps between levels are visible as steps rather than
  blurred into a slope.
- No slag or re-deposited film left after cleaning; brass re-deposits heavily.
- Surface finish inside the relief is consistent — a patch that is glossier or darker than the
  rest means the passes stopped cutting and started polishing.

## How to verify

Depth gauge at the deepest area and at one intermediate level, on the actual piece if it is a
one-off, otherwise on a coupon. Clean with isopropanol before judging finish. Look across the
piece at a low angle: unevenness in a relief floor shows at a glancing angle long before it shows
straight on.
