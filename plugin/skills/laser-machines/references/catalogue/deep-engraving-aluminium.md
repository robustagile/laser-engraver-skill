## Acceptance criteria

- Depth reaches the target the job states, at the shallowest point, within -0 / +30 %.
- Even floor across the whole marked area; no dished centre from the middle passes cutting
  deeper than the edges.
- No smeared or re-melted lip standing proud of the surface after cleaning.
- Aluminium re-deposits readily: the surface around the cut must clean up with isopropanol and a
  cloth, with no adherent grey film left behind.

## How to verify

Depth gauge at the centre and at two opposite ends of a solid cut, taking the shallowest. Clean
the coupon before measuring — an uncleaned re-deposit reads as less depth than there is.
