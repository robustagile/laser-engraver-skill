## Acceptance criteria

- Uniform dark grey to black over the whole area, with no colour fringing at the edges of the
  marked region — titanium shifts through straw, bronze and blue on the way to dark, and a
  fringe means the edge of the fill ran at a different effective dose from the middle.
- Zero material removal: depth gauge reads nothing, surface smooth to a fingernail.
- Survives an isopropanol wipe.

**Not on hand.** Where the owner has none of this material, the entry stays a `candidate` on
researched numbers and never reaches `verified` — the coverage map shows it as a gap, and the
agent must say plainly that nothing here was ever burned before anyone runs it on a part
(R-R18).

## How to verify

Depth gauge, then eye in daylight straight on and at 45 degrees, paying attention to the first
and last few millimetres of the fill rather than its middle. Isopropanol wipe.
