## Acceptance criteria

- The design's depth levels are distinguishable to the eye and to a fingernail.
- No spalling or flaking at the edges of the cut, and no cracking radiating from a corner.
- Coverage is even across the piece; natural stone varies in hardness and a setting that works at
  one end of a slate tile may not at the other.
- Dust clears with a brush and does not need scraping.

## How to verify

Fingernail and a low-angle look for the levels; a loupe at the corners for cracks. Burn the same
test at two places on the same tile — the point of the check is the variation within the material,
not a single good cell. Slate is the assumed grade; granite and marble need their own recipe.
