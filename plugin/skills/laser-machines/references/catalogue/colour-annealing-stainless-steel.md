## Acceptance criteria

- The colour the job asked for, recognisably and by name, not "some colour came out".
- **The same colour across the whole field.** A colour that is right at field centre and shifted
  at the corners is not verified — the incidence angle changes across a galvo field and colour is
  the work that shows it first.
- Zero material removal; surface smooth to a fingernail.
- Repeatable: the same settings on the next run give a colour that reads as the same one.
- Survives an isopropanol wipe and light rubbing with a cloth.

## How to verify

Burn the same swatch at field centre and at two opposite corners of the working area in one run,
and compare the three in daylight — this is the check that distinguishes a colour recipe from a
lucky cell. Repeat the whole run once. Colour is the one place where a photograph is worth
keeping alongside the text, for the owner's own reference, but the verdict is still the eye's
(R-R11).

Colour on stainless is a pulse-duration and frequency result above all: it is the work that most
needs the MOPA levers, and the reason a plain Q-switched fiber machine is excluded from this
entry.
